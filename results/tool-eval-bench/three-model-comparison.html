Strengths and weaknesses of GLM-5.3-Flash in this comparison

Strengths
  • Lowest variance of the three (±0.9) and the tightest reliability gap (10.3 pp)
  • Highest deployability (81) and fastest median turn (2.2 s) on 3× DGX Spark
  • Perfect Parameter Precision, Instruction Following, Creative Composition, Structured Output, Code Patterns
  • Best Hard Mode mean (83%)
Weaknesses
  • Multi-Step Chains 75% — one chain scenario (TC-62) fails in every trial
  • Autonomous Planning 79%, Structured Reasoning 83%, Restraint & Refusal 83%
  • Skips a required search/read step in several multi-turn state scenarios (TC-57, 63, 80, 82)
  • TC-43: empty required parameter instead of asking the user (the single safety warning)

Conclusion

On the published numbers, DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp keeps the top mean score (88.6 vs 87.8), while GLM-5.3-Flash NVFP4 on three DGX Spark nodes lands within one point of it with the lowest variance, the best reliability floor and gap, and the fastest turns, and clears Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4 by about 2.4 points. GLM's gap to DeepSeek is concentrated in multi-step chains and autonomous planning; its lead is in parameter precision, instruction following and structured output. Given the benchmark-version difference described above, the fair statement is: GLM-5.3-Flash is on par with DeepSeek-V4-Flash and clearly ahead of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on this suite.
